Who We Are

Rhema Word Ministries is an apostolic house led by Apostle Keith & Pastor Talent Bhehane, commissioned to declare the Word of God and manifest His wonders on Earth.

Apostle Keith Bhehane

Founder & Lead Pastor, Rhema Word Ministries

Leadership Bio

Apostle Keith Bhehane

Apostle Keith Bhehane is a dynamic and eloquent teacher of the Word of God, deeply passionate about seeing people prosper and be set free from every captivity in every sphere of life. He ministers the Word with rare simplicity and profound revelation — touching hearts, stirring faith, and demonstrating the healing and prophetic power of Christ.

Beside him stands his beautiful and loving wife, Pastor Talent Bhehane, a visionary pastor and ministry partner. Together, Keith and Talent have been married since 2012 and are proud parents of three sons — Jaden, Cayden and Nathan. Their family is a living testimony of God's faithfulness and grace.

Called with an apostolic mandate to reach the nations, Apostle Keith established the People's Foundation — a practical charity outreach serving as a beacon of hope, feeding and clothing thousands across Zimbabwe and beyond.

Our Beginnings: Rhema Word Ministries held its very first service on 08 October 2017 in a living room at No. 19 Cawstone Street, Northend, Bulawayo, with just 19 people. It closed twice before relaunching in 2020 in a house in Pelandaba. Within a few months, we moved to Esiziba Youth Center in Njube, and as the ministry grew, transitioned to Pelandaba Hall. On 02 July 2023, we moved to town at Phakama Building, and on 02 July 2026, we moved to our own venue in town, Luxor House Basement.
